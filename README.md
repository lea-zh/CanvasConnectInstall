# CanvasConnect

Mac app that connects Claude or Codex to your Figma canvas.

## Install

1. Click **CanvasConnect.dmg** above, then the download button.
2. Open it and drag **CanvasConnect** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open CanvasConnect from **Applications**.

## "CanvasConnect" Not Opened

The first time, macOS says Apple could not verify CanvasConnect. This is expected: the app isn't from the App Store or signed by Apple yet.

1. Click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and scroll down.
3. Next to "CanvasConnect was blocked", click **Open Anyway**, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

After that it opens normally. Always open it from Applications, not from the .dmg window.
